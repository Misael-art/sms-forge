Use case: original fighter concept-art reference for the planned Master of Fighters fan game; this is an illustration, not a runtime sprite.
Subject: Landau, the brave northern warrior and player hero from Lord of the Sword for Sega Master System. He is a young adult in his early twenties, clean-shaven, with smooth youthful features and no facial hair or age lines. Create an original side-view fighting-game interpretation with the project's provisional heavy build: broad, sturdy, muscular but agile, with grounded legs and clear martial balance. Keep his identity recognizable through the green tunic, long dark-brown hair, leather gear, sword, and bow equipment. Match the original game sprite's color family. The official box illustration is a secondary anatomy and costume reference; do not copy its pose or composition.
Composition: wide horizontal concept sheet, exactly two separate full-body poses of the same Landau side by side, both fully visible with generous transparent negative space. Left: three-quarter defensive guard, sword held low and ready, bow and quiver secured across his back. Right: decisive advancing sword cut, torso and hair swept by the motion, bow and quiver still visible. Keep the silhouettes distinct and make the sword readable in both poses. No extra characters, inset views, props, swatches, or labels.
Character design: young adult northern fantasy warrior, about 20–22, with shoulder-length dark-brown hair, a plain narrow leather headband, clean-shaven youthful face, sleeveless green tunic, practical brown leather shoulder and forearm guards, broad belt, blue cloth trousers, simple metal shin protection and sturdy boots. Keep his face smooth and free of wrinkles, beard, moustache, or stubble. A restrained red detail may echo the game sprite. His sword is plain silver steel with a small orange-gold hilt. Carry a simple wooden bow and a compact quiver on the back. He has not yet earned the crown: no crown, royal cape, ornate plate armor, or purple fabric.
Style: polished 1990s Japanese anime fighting-game concept illustration, bold controlled near-black ink contours, strong readable anatomy and silhouette, hard cel shading with three flat tones per major material, saturated Master System colors, dramatic rim light. Use smooth flat fills with a few deliberate hard-edged shadow regions.
Exact Master System palette only (6-bit channel steps of 85): opaque near-black outline #555555; skin #FFAAAA and #FF5555; shared warm brown #AA5500; olive hair shadow #555500 and deep brown #550000; scarlet accent #AA0000; green tunic #AAFF00, #55AA00, #005500; gold leather #FFAA00; steel #FFFFFF and #AAAAAA; blue cloth #AAAAFF and #5555AA. Pure black #000000 is reserved for transparent index 0 and must not appear in the figure. Final palette conversion will map all colors to these project swatches.
Materials: large clean uninterrupted color regions, three hard tones for skin, hair, tunic, leather, and steel; no dithering. The blue clothing can use its two declared blue tones. No gradients, soft airbrush, glow, bloom, paper texture, grain, speckles, noise, or scanned-paint effects. Transparent background.
Text: none.
Avoid: crown, purple clothing, white-haired design, transformation, fangs, monster features, full plate armor, extra weapons, background scenery, repeated pose, cropped body parts, extra palette colors, pure-black fills, text, logos, watermark.
