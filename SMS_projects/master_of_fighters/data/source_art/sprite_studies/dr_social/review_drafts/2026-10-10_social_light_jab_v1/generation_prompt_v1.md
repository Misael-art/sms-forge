## Prompt usado

Create a new original pixel-art animation sprite sheet candidate for Dr. Ferdinand Social, a slim adult human duelist in a 1990s arcade fighting game.

Use Reference 1 as the authority for his approved identity and silhouette; use Reference 2 only for clean cel-shaded color grouping. Keep his gray swept hair, clearly round brass spectacles, mature narrow face, teal/cyan tailcoat with large contiguous deep-teal shadows, burgundy cravat, white shirt and gloves, dark/navy outline, brown boots, and straight silver walking cane with round gold pommel. Same adult height/body mass in all frames. No chibi, bulky redesign, supernatural traits, magic, rapier, or boomerang.

Show exactly six full-body animation poses in a clean 3-column x 2-row sheet, same size, same fighting direction and baseline, one full pose per cell:

1. J01 guard with fist near chest and cane upright in rear/off-hand.
2. J02 anticipation: torso and punching shoulder visibly rotate back, elbow bends further, fist pulls toward the ribs.
3. J03 acceleration: fist is exactly halfway between J02 and full extension, elbow still slightly bent.
4. J04 impact: one compact straight punch at shoulder height, arm fully but naturally extended, wrist straight; this is the only impact/contact pose.
5. J05 recoil: elbow visibly bends and fist is midway from J04 back toward guard; cuff and coat hem lag slightly.
6. J06 exact recovered guard matching J01.

Make J01, J02, J03, J04, J05, J06 visually distinct at a glance; do not duplicate any neighboring pose. Show subtle motion by changed body/arm poses only, no effects.

Background: every empty pixel in the whole sheet is exactly one flat pure magenta key color RGB(255,0,255). No transparency, no gradients, no shadows, no halo, no vignette, no glow, no texture, no floor, no cell dividers. No labels, words, numbers, border, UI, or watermark. The magenta must remain uniform and must not appear on the character.

The cane stays in the same hand for all six poses. Keep hand wrapped around the handle, shaft straight, pommel round, tip visible, complete, and inside the cell. The punch arm must remain connected shoulder→upper arm→elbow→forearm→wrist→glove in every pose. Keep both feet and cane tip inside each frame. Consistent anatomy and clothing design across all six cells.

Crisp hard-edged pixel clusters, limited palette with at most 15 opaque character colors, uniform dark outline, flat high-contrast cel-shaded blocks. No dithering, antialiasing, blur, gradients, grain, noise, confetti pixels, loose outline pixels, or smoothing. Pixel-art sprite sheet for review, not a poster.
