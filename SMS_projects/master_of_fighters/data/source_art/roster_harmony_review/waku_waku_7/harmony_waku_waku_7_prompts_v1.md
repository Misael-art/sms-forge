# Waku Waku 7 roster harmony — prompt set v1

## Style target
Waku Waku 7-inspired 1990s Sunsoft/Neo Geo manga fighter illustration: bold clean dark contours, saturated but controlled character colors, crisp cel-shaded color groups, bright highlights and expressive arcade-fighter presence. One shared line weight and rendering finish; retain each character’s identity, outfit colors, gear and established body type. No chibi redesign, no bulk redesign, no copied Waku Waku characters.

## Targeted refinements and layout
The generator initially drew a checkerboard background and approximate scale. The checkerboard was replaced with neutral cream. Follow-up edits aligned Joe/Dr. Social and corrected the Zillion pistol silhouette and orientation. The final layout applies uniform proportional scaling from crown-to-sole body measurements to enforce the numeric targets; equipment above the head does not define character height. Names, U ruler, baseline and review status are typeset in the final sheets.

## Avoid
Extra figures, swapped palettes, copied Waku Waku characters, chibi heads, bulky redesign, fused limbs, floating hands, unclear grips, incorrectly colored toy gun, checkerboard, gradients, texture, grain, blur, pseudo-text, logos and watermark.
