 PSGlib 
 Overview 
 A Z80 ASM library for SN76489 background music with SFX support. Basically, this plays a (converted) VGM and, on top of that, can play another (converted) VGM that uses only some of the channels, leaving the other channels undisturbed. You can create your SN76489 VGMs using either Mod2PSG2 , Deflemask , VGM Music Maker or Furnace and there may be other tools I never heard of.
 SFXs can also be generated using the amazing sn_sfxr sound effects generator for instance, and later edited in Mod2PSG2 as this tool also supports importing VGMs.
